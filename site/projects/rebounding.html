NBA Rebound Predictor

Sketch a play at the moment of a shot and the model predicts where each player moves and how likely they are to grab the rebound.

  • Python
  • Flask
  • Keras
  • scikit-learn
  • D3.js
Left-click offense · right-click defense · shift-click the shooter

How to use it

  • Left-click to drop an offensive player (up to 5)
  • Right-click to drop a defender (up to 5)
  • Shift-click, or the 5th offensive click, marks the shooter (gold ring)
  • Hit Run — players animate to predicted positions
  • Color runs blue → red by rebound likelihood; hover a player for its probability

The demo calls /api/rebound/predict. Running locally? Start the Flask app and set window.REBOUND_CONFIG.endpoint below to http://localhost:8000/predict.

How it works

The predictor runs in two learning stages. A small neural network (Keras) takes the pre-shot positions of all ten players and predicts each player's post-shot position — rebounding is about movement toward the ball, not just where you start. Those predicted positions feed a feature step, and a random forest (scikit-learn) turns the features into a rebound probability for each player, normalized across the ten to sum to one.

Engineered features

  • Distance & angle to the hoop — geometry relative to the rim.
  • Cosine similarity to the shooter's angle — are you on the shot's line to the basket?
  • Box-out count — a single k-means-style assignment estimates how many opponents each player screens off.
  • Movement vector — magnitude and direction the position model expects, signed by whether the player gets closer to the rim.

Front-end

The court is a D3.js overlay on an SVG half-court. Clicks build a payload of player coordinates, a POST hits the Flask service, and the JSON response drives the color gradient and the transition animation, outlining the single most likely rebounder.
