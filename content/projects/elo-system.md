This is the final iteration of an R script I wrote years ago to manually calculate our
league's Elo ratings. Now a fully fleshed out Python package, you can connect it to your
fantasy league and calculate ratings based on completed matchups. If your league is a dynasty
league you can get both the seasonal and all time ratings. You can adjust the K factor or
off-season equilibrium rate to fit your league as needed.

## What it supports

**Platforms** — Fantrax, Sleeper

**Leagues** — NBA, NFL

**Formats** — Categories, Points
